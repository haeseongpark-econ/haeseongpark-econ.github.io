Teaching Approach

Teaching Philosophy

My goal as an instructor is to help students develop economic intuition while giving them the analytical tools needed to evaluate real-world questions. I emphasize clear explanations, structured problem solving, and connections between economic theory and empirical evidence.

I aim to create an inclusive learning environment in which students feel comfortable asking questions, testing ideas, and building confidence in their ability to use economics independently.
