About

Hi! My name is Haeseong Park. I’m a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Colorado, Boulder.

My current work research examines how natural disasters reshape economic geography across the U.S., from the way people move to how communities bounce back.

I will be on the 2026–27 academic job market.
